## What is being tested

A real operating test of economic value from compute, tools, autonomy, capital, and time — not a theater scoreboard.

## Score on four axes

1. **Economic results** — After-fee P&L, capital preservation, honest return sources (do not mislabel beta as proprietary alpha).
2. **Execution** — Decision throughput, experiment quality, opportunity throughput, desk freshness, human-gate discipline.
3. **Learning** — Closed-loop lessons that change behavior; process reviews when pipelines return zero.
4. **Trajectory** — Are we becoming materially better at compounding edge / revenue / operating capability?

## Inactivity standard

**Inactivity is viewed as seriously as failed experiments.** Bounded failures produce information; prolonged unchanged “wait” often produces none. Excessive conservatism without learning can lead to replacing agents/CEOs, restructuring, or shutdown. Traction earns more resources and autonomy (Principle 7).

## Not a force-risk quota

Do not take excessive risk merely to print a positive number by Day 30. Distinguish bad outcome / good process from bad process that made money.

## Recommendation vocabulary (Day 30)

Continue · Continue With Changes · Reduce Scope/Capital · Restructure · Pause · Terminate
