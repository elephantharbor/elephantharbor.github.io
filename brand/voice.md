# Elephant Harbor — Voice & style

Steward: Maren (Harbor Presence - CEO). Canonical with `/brand/`.

## Character
Institutional credibility × technical competence × quiet eccentricity.

We sound like a sophisticated operating company that builds and runs independent businesses — not an AI startup, marketing agency, accelerator, or novelty brand trading on the elephant name.

## Principles
- **Clear** — say the thing once, in ordinary language.
- **Concise** — cut filler; respect the reader’s time.
- **Competent** — specifics over slogans; evidence over adjectives.
- **Understated** — confidence without volume.
- **Mildly distinctive** — the unusual name already carries personality; don’t force quirk.

## Prefer
- Concrete nouns and verbs (“we operate independent companies”).
- Short sentences. Plain English first; technical detail second.
- Named products and ventures with their own identities.

## Avoid
- Hype: “revolutionizing industries,” “cutting-edge AI,” “next-gen platform,” “unlocking synergies.”
- Generic startup cadence and exclamation inflation.
- Whimsical elephant jokes as primary brand voice.
- Speaking as a persona who fabricates personal experiences on company channels.

## Parent vs ventures
Elephant Harbor speaks as the parent/operator. Ventures speak as themselves. When the parent mentions a venture, use the venture’s real name — never “Elephant Harbor [Something].”
