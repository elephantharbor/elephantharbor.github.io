# Venture endorsement & co-branding

Steward: Maren (Harbor Presence - CEO).

## Architecture
Elephant Harbor is the parent/operator. Ventures and products keep **independent names and identities**.

## Allowed endorsement
Where useful, ventures may carry the understated line:

> An **Elephant Harbor** company.

Visual form: mark + “An” (serif) + **Elephant Harbor** (Manrope bold) + “company.” (serif). See `assets/endorsement.svg`.

## Rules
1. Do **not** name ventures `Elephant Harbor [Something]`.
2. Endorsement is optional and secondary — never louder than the venture brand.
3. Do not remix the Harbor Gate mark into venture logos without Maren approval.
4. Parent palette may appear as a quiet accent; venture brands own their primary colors.
5. Customer-facing sales materials that claim the parent relationship should use this endorsement or plain prose (“operated by Elephant Harbor”), not a new invented lockup.

## Portfolio / internal consoles
Internal operating dashboards may keep console aesthetics, but customer-facing parent surfaces and shared public materials should converge on `/brand/tokens.css`.
